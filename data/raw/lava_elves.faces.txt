# Face 1 is the ID face, added here: the source lists the other faces only. Its count is the
# unit's health, 4 on a monster (invariant 7). The monster faces carry no count in the source
# and are written as 4: right for every normal icon (invariant 7), but an SAI's count can be
# its X instead -- Firewalkers' Gorgon prints '2 Flame' -- so a monster SAI's 4 is unconfirmed.
# Flame is confirmed as 2 on the Beholder and Hell Hound; the other monster SAIs' 4 stays unconfirmed.
# Other faces are in the source's order. Headers are '<class>-<size>' / 'monster-<name>',
# with the class lines as the source names them; the v4.01 roster is p. 84.
# Anything marked TODO disagrees with the shape of a die and needs checking against one.
