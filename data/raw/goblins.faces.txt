# Face 1 is the ID face, added here: the source lists the other faces only. Its count is the
# unit's health, 4 on a monster (invariant 7). The monster faces carry no count in the source

# Other faces are in the source's order. Headers are '<class>-<size>' / 'monster-<name>',
# with the class lines as the source names them; the v4.01 roster is p. 82.
# Anything marked TODO disagrees with the shape of a die and needs checking against one.
