What the frames decide

A card, top to bottom

  • Step bar: the exchange, not the card. It sits on every card and every decision sheet of one exchange, so a Flame's target sheet and the saves card after it share one bar.
  • Heading: whose roll, what for, where. "The enemy rolls melee at Your home". The total sits at the right of it, on every card, live or logged.
  • Strip: every die with its count and icon. A die that scored nothing is dashed and quiet, never blank. An SAI die has the SAI's name under it.
  • Arithmetic: mathPhrase, on its own line, and the outcome in "you" and "the enemy".
  • One line per SAI that resolves: the die, what it does in effectSummary's words, and when ("next", "after Sleep").
  • Foot: Continue. "roll 1 of 2" is gone: the bar says where you are.

The step bar

  • Four steps, named by the action: Melee/Missile/Counter → SAIs → Saves → Result; Maneuver → SAIs → Contest → Result; Magic → SAIs → Spells.
  • A step with nothing in it is drawn struck through, not hidden, so the bar's shape is fixed per action.
  • SAIs are chips on the step whose roll showed them: the attack's on SAIs, a save roll's Cantrip, Choke or Confuse on Saves, Rapid Growth on Contest.
  • A chip is 16px tall, under the 44px tap floor. It shows the order. It is not where the order is chosen.

The board waits (9c)

  • Mark, don't rewind. A die killed, moved or put to sleep in an entry past the cursor is drawn where it was, as if nothing happened.
  • On the card that resolves it, it is marked on the board as well (falls), so the card and the board point at one die.
  • After that card it is gone. The ticker reads the entry at the cursor, never the newest.
