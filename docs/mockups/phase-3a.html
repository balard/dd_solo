What the armies are

  • Mid-game: a mix of sizes, a few dice in each Reserve Army, some dead in the DUA, the Frontier held by you and one enemy dragon summoned there. At 12 health the force is data/forces/mixed-12.json, so your side is mixed Treefolk and Firewalkers.
  • All small: setup, every die a 1-health small, three equal armies.
  • Setup worst: all small, Home and Horde at the half-force cap and a 2-die Campaign. The enemy's Horde is at your Home: 34 dice there at 36 health.
  • Pile-up: both whole forces, all small, at the Frontier. Impossible at setup and possible in play: 72 dice in one terrain at 36 health. Every dragon is there, two of your dice are asleep, and eight spells are active: three on each army and two on the place.

The landscape board

  • One row per terrain: your army left, the terrain die in the middle, the enemy right, both packed toward the die.
  • Ownership sits on the owner's side of the die: a solid tag on your side, an outlined one on the enemy's. HOME for a Home Terrain, HELD for a captured eighth face, which also draws the die with a double border.
  • A fourth row for the Reserve Armies, laid out the same way, since a Reserve Army marches most turns.
  • The DUA, the BUA and the Summoning Pool are counts at each side's outer edge of the reserve row, and open on a tap. The side rail is gone.
  • Dragons are tokens under the die, in the element's colour, D or W for drake or wyrm.
  • Effects are counted chips over the army they sit on. A terrain effect gets a dashed chip across the whole row.
  • The log is the one-line ticker from 3b in both layouts.

Tile sizes

  • Today is TILE_SIZE (48/54/60/70), Compact keeps the 44px tap target (44/46/50/56), Tight goes under it (36/38/42/48).
  • Shapes drops the badge and the digit: the class shape alone, 22/25/28/32 by health, so size still reads as health. At that size a tap needs a zoom step to pick one die.
  • Auto starts every terrain at Today and steps down the tallest one at a time until the board fits or everything is shapes. Both armies at one terrain share a size, so they stay comparable.
  • Stacks: identical dice become one tile with ×N on its corner. Identical means the same unit type in the same state: a sleeping die, or one under an effect aimed at that unit, gets its own tile. When crowded puts stacking into Auto's ladder right after Compact (Today → Compact → Compact stacked → Tight stacked → Shapes stacked), so it is tried before any tile drops under the 44px tap target.

Screens

  • Laptop: 1366 × 680, a 768-pixel screen less the browser's own bars.
  • Desktop: 1920 × 960.
  • Phone sideways: 844 × 390, the installed app on an iPhone 14-class phone. In a browser tab it loses about 50 more.
  • Phone upright: 390 × 844, for comparison. Landscape is not meant for it.
